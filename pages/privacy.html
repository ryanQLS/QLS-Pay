Privacy Policy

QLS Pay is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect information necessary to provide our payment advisory services. Your personal information is used solely to review your payment processing costs and provide recommendations. We do not share your merchant statements or financial information with third parties without your consent.

Information We Collect

When you submit a merchant statement for review, we collect:

  • Your business email address
  • Your merchant statement or payment processing details
  • Transaction history and fee information
  • Business type and industry classification

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Analyze your current payment processing costs
  • Identify potential savings opportunities
  • Provide personalized rate audit recommendations
  • Contact you with our findings and recommendations

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your merchant statements are treated as confidential business information.

Contact Us

For more information about how we handle your data or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us at info@qlspay.ca.
